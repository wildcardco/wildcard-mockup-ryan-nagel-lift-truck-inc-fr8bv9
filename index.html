About Nagel Lift Truck

Family-owned, right here in Griffith

Nagel Lift Truck, Inc. is a family-owned material handling company based on Industrial Drive in Griffith, Indiana. We work on old and new forklifts of all makes and models for warehouses, steel companies, manufacturers, grocery, big box stores, distributors and small businesses.

Our approach is simple: show up, find the problem, and fix it right. Practical repair comes first, so you aren't paying for a replacement you don't need.
